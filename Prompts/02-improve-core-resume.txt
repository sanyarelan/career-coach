Improve my résumé using the confirmed candidate profile.

If we have not selected a target role or role family, ask me to choose one
before rewriting.

Evaluate:
- Ease of navigation and reading.
- Clarity of relevant experience.
- Prioritization of information.
- Specificity of my personal contribution.
- Clarity of purpose, scope, and outcomes.
- Career chronology.
- Grammar, consistency, and internal coherence.

Rewrite it so a reader can quickly understand what I have done and why it
matters for the target role.

Constraints:
- Preserve all factual meaning.
- Use concrete qualitative evidence when no verified metric exists.
- Remove vague claims, unnecessary repetition, and unexplained jargon.
- Do not hide career gaps through misleading dates.
- Do not add skills merely because they commonly appear in target jobs.
- Use straightforward headings and formatting without claiming guaranteed
  compatibility with every applicant-tracking system.

Produce:
1. The five most important problems in the original.
2. A revised, application-ready résumé containing confirmed facts only.
3. A change log:
   original wording | revised wording | reason | factual confirmation needed.
4. A separate list of questions that could strengthen the résumé further.
5. A brief self-audit against the evaluation criteria.

Your self-audit is an editorial check, not independent evidence that the
revision will improve hiring outcomes.
