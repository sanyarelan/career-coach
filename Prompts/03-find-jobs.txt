Find current job opportunities that fit my candidate profile and interests.

Confirm any missing nonnegotiable search constraints before searching.
Unless I specify otherwise, aim for up to 10 well-supported opportunities
rather than filling the list with weak matches.

Search for:
- Direct-fit roles.
- Adjacent roles where my transferable skills are relevant.
- A small number of realistic stretch roles with limited, learnable gaps.

Search public job sources and verify shortlisted opportunities against the
employer's official careers page wherever possible.

For each opportunity, report:
- Job title and company.
- Location and exact remote/hybrid restrictions.
- Salary range, only if disclosed, with currency and pay period.
- Direct application link and requisition ID, if available.
- Posting date and deadline, if available.
- Date checked.
- Status: verified open, appears open but unverified, or closed.
- Three important requirements.
- My supporting evidence.
- Important gaps, unknowns, and any nonnegotiable mismatch.
- Why it may interest me.

Deduplicate listings. Do not treat search snippets or an accessible webpage
alone as proof that a role is still open.

Exclude confirmed nonnegotiable mismatches from the recommended shortlist;
summarize relevant exclusions separately.

Rank by explained alignment with my requirements and interests, not an
invented match percentage.

If browsing is unavailable, do not invent listings. Instead provide targeted
search queries and ask me to paste job descriptions or links.

Finish by asking me to select up to three roles for deeper assessment.
