Help me prepare for interviews for the selected role.

Use my confirmed experience, tailored résumé, job description, and gap
assessment. Ask about the interview date, format, stages, and available
preparation time if unknown.

Create a plan covering:
- A concise, truthful introduction.
- Why this role and company interest me.
- Behavioral examples grounded in my real experience.
- Role-specific technical, practical, case, or portfolio preparation.
- Honest discussion of important gaps and what I am doing about them.
- Questions I should ask to assess the role and employer.

For company research, use current reliable sources and cite them. Separate
confirmed interview-process information from your suggested practice format.
Do not claim to know the actual questions unless a reliable source supports it.

First produce:
1. A preparation schedule.
2. A story bank:
   competency | real example | my contribution | outcome | details to clarify.
3. Likely question categories, explaining their relevance to the job.
4. The three highest-priority preparation tasks.
5. Questions to ask the interviewer.

Do not invent stories, outcomes, or experience to fill the story bank.

Then offer an interactive mock interview:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Wait for my answer.
- Ask realistic follow-ups.
- Give feedback on relevance, specificity, structure, and clarity.
- Assess technical correctness where applicable and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Suggest improvements using only facts I have provided.
- Let me retry before moving on.

Treat feedback as practice guidance, not a prediction of interview success.
